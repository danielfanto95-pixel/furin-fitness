I've Been There Too

Hi, I'm Daniel.

At my heaviest, I weighed around 260 pounds.

I know what it's like to look in the mirror and want something different. I know what it's like to wonder where to start. And I know what it's like to make fitness much more complicated than it needs to be.

So I changed. I lost the weight. I learned how to train. I learned nutrition. I built muscle. And over the years, fitness completely transformed my body.

But eventually, I experienced the other extreme of fitness too — more tracking, more cardio, more dieting, more worrying about the scale, more fear that doing less would somehow mean losing progress.

That taught me something important: fitness can improve your life, but it can also take over your life if you let it.

Most people don't need the extreme version. You don't need to become a fitness expert. You don't need to spend hours working out. You don't need to obsess over every calorie. You need to know what to do, how much to do, whether it's working, and what to change when it isn't.

That realization is a huge part of why I built Furin Fitness — I want to help you get the body you want without having to learn everything the hard way.

You want to lose fat, build muscle, and finally feel confident in your body. But actually getting there can feel overwhelming.

Frequently Asked Questions

Still Have Questions?

Do I need a gym?

Not necessarily. Your training can be designed around the equipment and environment available to you.

Do I have to completely change my diet?

No. The goal is to build your nutrition around your life instead of making your entire life revolve around your diet.

What if I've never worked out before?

That's completely fine. You don't have to get in shape before getting help getting in shape — that's the whole point.

What if I'm really out of shape?

We start from where you are — not where you think you should already be.

What if I'm intimidated by the gym?

That's exactly the kind of fear we're trying to remove. You don't need to walk into a gym already knowing everything — that's the point of having guidance.

What if I've tried losing weight before?

We'll look at what caused previous attempts to break down and build around those barriers instead of blindly starting another diet.

What if I'm afraid I'll fail again?

You don't need a perfect record. You need a plan for what happens when things aren't perfect.

What if my schedule changes?

We adjust your plan.

Is this really less than 30 minutes a day?

Furin Fitness is designed around making your fitness commitment as simple and time-efficient as possible, with training generally targeted at less than 30 minutes per day. Your exact needs may vary depending on your individual goals and starting point.

Will I need coaching forever?

That's not the goal. I want you to understand fitness well enough that eventually you can confidently manage it yourself.

What happens during the Fitness Game Plan Session?

We'll spend about 15 minutes talking through your goals, previous attempts, lifestyle, schedule, barriers, fears, and concerns. Then I'll use that to create your customized starting game plan.

Do I have to sign up for coaching to get my game plan?

No. If we decide coaching makes sense, I'll explain how Furin Fitness can help you execute it. But the brainstorming session and customized game plan come first.
